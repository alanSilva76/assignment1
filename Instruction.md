## Assignment 1: Vitals

### Details

As part of a hospital vital signs display, create a row of boxes with vital stats in them:

* Pulse (beats per minute)
* Blood Oxygen Level (%)
* Avg. Sleep time (hours)

The numbers should be large so they can be read from across the room. Be sure to include labels (“Pulse”) and units (bpm; % -- they can be smaller).

### Optional: Take it further

* Make a second version that scales the whole UI to fill the screen.
* Think about other applications (non-medical) that might use such a display.
